Does your puppy bite your hands? The AKC method

Published September 6, 2026 · Reviewed September 16, 2026

Puppies bite to explore. They still need to learn it hurts.

Mouthing is normal puppy behavior: it is how puppies play and explore. The American Kennel Club recommends teaching bite inhibition early.

When teeth touch skin, stop the game. Stay still and quiet for a few seconds, then start again, and offer a toy your puppy is allowed to bite.

What you can do

  • Keep chew toys within reach during play.
  • Avoid games that encourage biting hands or feet.
