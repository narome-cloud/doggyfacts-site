The 1-hour-per-month puppy bladder rule

Published September 12, 2026 · Reviewed September 16, 2026

A simple rule of thumb to build a potty schedule that works.

The American Kennel Club's house-training guidance gives a useful rule of thumb: a puppy can usually hold its bladder for about one hour for every month of age. An 8-week-old puppy (about 2 months) needs a bathroom break roughly every 2 hours.

Accidents inside usually mean the puppy simply waited longer than it could. Building your schedule around this rule prevents most of them.

What you can do

  • Take your puppy out right after it wakes up, eats or plays.
  • Reward the moment your puppy goes outside, not after you walk back in.
  • Clean accidents with an enzymatic cleaner so the smell doesn't invite repeats.

Sources

  • American Kennel Club – How to Potty Train a Puppy
