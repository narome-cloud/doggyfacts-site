About Doggy Facts

An independent educational project that turns dog research into short, easy videos.

Doggy Facts started in September 2026 in Buenos Aires, Argentina, with a simple idea: dog owners ask the same questions every day, and the answers already exist in good research. They are just hard to find and hard to read.

We pick the questions people search for most, find a reliable source, and explain the answer in a video you can watch in under a minute. Our audience is English-speaking dog owners.

What we believe

  • Sources first. Every claim should be traceable to a study or a trusted organization.
  • Honesty about AI. We use AI tools to produce visuals and narration, and we say so on every video.
  • Kind training. We share reward-based methods and never recommend punishment or harmful equipment.
  • Not a replacement for a vet. For health or serious behavior problems, see a veterinarian or a certified professional.
