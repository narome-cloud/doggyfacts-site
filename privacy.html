Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Doggy Facts ("we") operates the website doggyfacts and the Doggy Facts publishing tool, an internal application used to publish our own videos to our own social media accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

What data we access

The publishing tool connects only to social media accounts owned by Doggy Facts. Through the platforms' official APIs (YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram Graph API) it accesses: basic profile information of our own accounts, the ability to upload and publish videos to those accounts, and performance statistics (views, likes, comments) of our own videos. It does not access data of any other user, follower or viewer.

How we use it

Solely to publish our videos and to measure how they perform so we can decide what to produce next. Access tokens are stored encrypted in our private infrastructure and are never shared or sold.

Website visitors

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal data from visitors.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use and can be revoked at any time from each platform's security settings. To request deletion of any data or to ask a question, write to narome@gmail.com.

Third-party platforms

Use of YouTube is subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok and Instagram are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.
