How a Doggy Facts video is made

1. Real questionsWe pick topics from what dog owners actually search for on Google and YouTube.
2. Real sourcesEach script is built on a peer-reviewed study or guidance from the AKC or VCA. No source, no video.
3. Clear visualsVisuals are created with AI tools and always labeled as AI-generated on every platform.

Why Doggy Facts

There is a lot of dog advice online, and much of it has no source. We keep each video short, stick to one idea, and link the research behind it so you can read more.

Our content is educational and does not replace advice from a veterinarian or a certified trainer. If something about your dog's health or behavior worries you, talk to a professional.
