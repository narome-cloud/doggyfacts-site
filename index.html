About this site

Doggy Facts is an independent educational channel. Our videos are scripted from peer-reviewed studies and reputable sources (AKC, VCA) and produced with the help of AI tools; every video discloses this. This website is also the home of the Doggy Facts publishing tool, an internal application that uploads our own videos to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.
